Best Practices

  • Daily Backup

    NEXTERP automatically backs up data. Verify backups are running.

  • Role-Based Access

    Assign appropriate roles to users. Don't share admin accounts.

  • Approval Workflows

    Use multi-step approvals for important transactions.

  • Regular Reconciliation

    Reconcile accounts monthly for accuracy.
